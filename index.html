01 Understand

What breast cancer is.

Abnormal cells in breast tissue grow out of control.

1

Who can develop it

Anyone, including men, although it is far more common in women.

2

Risk factors

Age, family history, inherited genetic variants, and other established factors.

3

Can risk be reduced?

Some risk factors can be changed, but not every case can be prevented.
